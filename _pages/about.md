I’m Addison Lin, a computer science undergraduate at the University of Oklahoma.

I enjoy building with code and exploring how software can turn an idea into something useful. My [projects](/projects/) include AI tools, a financial dashboard, and a browser-based file sharing application.

Outside of programming, you’ll usually find me playing tennis, traveling, taking photographs, or looking for good food. I keep some of those memories in my [photo albums](/gallery/) and [writing](/blog/).
